Projects

I have been involved in research projects focusing on knowledge graphs, artificial intelligence, and data privacy.

  • AI:HealthData 2026 - ongoing
    Principal investigator · Aalborg University AI:X

    We investigate how unstructured health records can be turned into actionable knowledge by combining AI and knowledge graphs to extract and organise clinical data for efficient analytics and trustworthy patient summaries.

    website
  • HEREDITARY 2024 - ongoing
    WP leader and AAU site lead · Horizon Europe

    We investigate privacy-preserving federated analytics by combining knowledge graphs and medical data to support the study of brain and gut-brain diseases.

    website
  • Digital Solutions Supporting Integrated Care Pathways for Atrial Fibrillation Patients 2025
    Principal investigator · Aalborg University Health Mission Seed Funding

    We explored integrated care pathways for atrial fibrillation patients from medical, ethical, and technological perspectives, supporting interdisciplinary dialogue and the identification of key challenges and opportunities.

    website
  • Knowledge Graphs and GNNs for Protein Analysis 2023
    · AI Denmark

    We explored how knowledge graphs and graph machine learning can be combined to analyse protein data and support biological discovery, in collaboration with Biogenity.

  • Privacy-Preserving Stream Analytics 2017 - 2021
    Project participant · Swiss National Science Foundation

    We developed privacy-preserving stream analytics methods to process large-scale data in real time, with applications in media platforms and radio astronomy.

    website
  • Social Effects of Artificial Intelligence 2018 - 2020
    Project participant · TA-Swiss

    We contributed to a multidisciplinary study on the societal impact of artificial intelligence across domains including education, ethics, law, media, and technology.

    website
  • INDENICA 2011 - 2012
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We contributed to the design of goal-oriented requirements engineering methods and tools for Platform-as-a-Service systems, including the development of the IRENE language and IRET editor.

    website
  • PlanetData 2011 - 2012
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We explored how games with a purpose can improve data quality, developing Urbanopoly to enhance geographic data in smart city applications.

    website
  • PANDORA 2010 - 2012
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We investigated how semantic web technologies can support the construction of knowledge bases for simulation-based learning systems.

    website
  • LarKC 2008 - 2011
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We applied Semantic Web techniques to urban data, enabling the collection, analysis, and modelling of city information for applications such as traffic prediction and recommendation systems.

    website
  • SOA4All 2009 - 2011
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We developed knowledge-based recommender systems for Web services, combining semantic technologies with collaborative and content-based approaches.

    website
  • Service Finder 2009 - 2010
    Project participant · EU FP7

    We investigated user profiling and recommendation techniques to support service discovery in Web service platforms.

    website
